Privacy Policy

Last updated: 20 November 2026

Prompt Haven respects your privacy. This page explains what data we collect, what we do not collect, and how cookies and ads work on this site. Please read it in full. If you do not agree with this policy, please do not use this site.

Who we are

Prompt Haven is a free website that shares AI photo editing prompts. The site is run by its owner, and the prompts are written by Muhammad Afaq. The contact email on the Contact page is a placeholder at the moment, and it will be replaced with a real email address when one is in place. This policy will be updated at that time if the contact details change.

What we collect

We do not ask you to create an account, sign up, or give us your name, email, or photo to read the prompts on this site. You can read every guide without giving us any personal data.

When you visit this site, basic technical data may be collected by the hosting service, Cloudflare Pages, and by your browser. This can include the page you visited, the time of your visit, the type of device and browser you used, and a general location based on your internet address. This data is used to keep the site working, to fix errors, and to understand which pages are read most. It is not used to identify you as a person.

We do not collect your photos. You do not upload a photo to Prompt Haven. If you use a prompt, you upload your photo to the AI tool you choose, such as Google Gemini or ChatGPT. That tool has its own privacy policy, and you should read it before you upload a photo there.

Google Analytics

We plan to use Google Analytics 4 (GA4) in the future to understand which guides are read most and how readers use the site. At the moment, no GA4 ID has been added to this site, and no GA4 tracking code is running. When a real GA4 ID is added, this section will be updated to show the ID and to explain what GA4 collects. GA4 may use cookies to count visits and to see which pages are read. You can block cookies in your browser if you do not want them.

Google AdSense and ads

At the moment, this site does not show Google AdSense ads, and no AdSense code has been added. We may apply for Google AdSense in the future. If the site is approved, ads may appear on some pages. Google AdSense may use cookies to show ads that fit the page you are reading. Google may also use data from your visit to show ads on other sites. You can read more in the Google Privacy Policy and in the Google Ad Settings page. We will update this policy when AdSense is added, and we will never claim that the site is approved by AdSense until it is.

Cookies

A cookie is a small file that a website can store on your device. This site may use cookies in the future for basic functions, for analytics, and for ads. At the moment, the site itself does not set tracking cookies, because no analytics or ads code is running. Your browser may still store cookies from the hosting service. You can delete cookies and block cookies in your browser settings. If you block cookies, the site will still work, and you can still read and copy prompts.

Data we do not sell

We do not sell your personal data. We do not share your personal data with other sites for marketing. If ads or analytics are added in the future, the ad and analytics providers may collect data under their own policies, and you can read those policies on their sites.

Your rights in Europe and the United Kingdom (GDPR)

If you are in Europe or the United Kingdom, you have rights under the General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR). You can ask what personal data we hold about you, ask us to correct it, ask us to delete it, and ask us to limit how we use it. Because this site does not ask you to sign up or give us your name or email, we usually hold no personal data that can identify you. If you send us an email, we will use your email only to reply to you, and we will not share it with others. You can ask us to delete your email at any time by writing to us again.

Your rights in California and the United States (CCPA)

If you are in California, you have rights under the California Consumer Privacy Act (CCPA). You can ask what personal data we collect, ask us to delete it, and ask us not to sell it. We do not sell personal data. Because this site does not ask you to sign up, we usually hold no personal data that can identify you. If you send us an email, we will use it only to reply to you.

Children (COPPA)

This site is not made for children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal data from children under 13. The prompts on this site include baby and birthday photos, but those prompts are for parents and guardians to use with their own photos. If you are under 13, please do not send us an email or give us any personal data. If you are a parent or guardian and you think a child has sent us personal data, please write to us and we will delete it.

Links to other sites

This site links to Google Gemini, ChatGPT, and Grok, so you can open the tool and paste a prompt. Those sites have their own privacy policies, and we are not responsible for what they collect. Please read their policies before you upload a photo there.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy when the site changes, for example when a real contact email, a GA4 ID, or AdSense is added. The date at the top of this page will change when we update it. Please check this page from time to time.

Contact

If you have a question about this policy, please use the Contact page. The email there is a placeholder at the moment, and it will be replaced with a real email address when one is in place.
