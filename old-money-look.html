Old Money Look Prompts: 10 Ideas for Gemini and ChatGPT 2026

Muhammad Afaq Verified Creator
Published: 2026-11-05 | Trending Prompts | Reading time: about 12 minutes
Start here: my pick

Try Prompt 3 first, the white shirt on a plain background. It sounds too simple, I know. But my blazer tries kept coming out like a costume, while the white shirt just looked like me on a good day. Start simple, then try the blazer.

Old money style is not about showing off. It is the opposite. Plain colours, good fabric, nothing loud. A blazer that fits, a white shirt, a book in hand. Quiet, and sure of itself.

I like that calm, but my first tries were a mess. I asked for too much at once, a car, a garden, a dog, and the tool gave me a magazine shoot with a stranger in it.

What worked was stripping it back. One clear photo, one classic outfit, one simple setting. A garden, a library, a country house, a park in a smart coat.

Start with the white shirt if you are new to this look. It shows you what the style is about in a single picture, and it is the hardest one to get wrong.

Before you start

This look is all about restraint, so these five points matter. A busy photo will fight the style and win.

  • Use a photo where your face is clear and facing the camera. The style is calm, and a clear face keeps it that way.
  • Pick a starting photo with a simple background. A plain wall or an open garden suits this look better than a busy room.
  • Keep your expression relaxed. A small smile or a neutral look fits the classic mood better than a big grin.
  • Copy the prompt exactly as it is the first time. Change one thing later if you need to, not five at once.
  • Save the best result, then try once more. Soft light varies a little each run, and the second version is often the cleaner one.

How to use these prompts

Style photos are about a look, so keep the starting photo simple and let the prompt add the clothes, the setting and the mood.

How to use a prompt in Google Gemini

  1. Open Gemini on your phone or computer.
  2. Upload a clear photo. Your face should be in focus and facing the camera.
  3. Copy one prompt from this page and paste it under your photo.
  4. Send it, and wait for the result. Do not send the same prompt again while it is still working.
  5. Look at the face first, then the outfit and the background. If the face looks right but the style feels too strong, try the same prompt once more.

How to use a prompt in ChatGPT

  1. Open ChatGPT on your phone or computer.
  2. Upload your photo in the chat.
  3. Copy one prompt from this page and paste it with your photo.
  4. Send it, and wait for the result.
  5. If the result is close but not quite right, ask for one small change, such as softer light or a plainer background, and keep the rest of the prompt the same.

Both tools gave me a different result from the same prompt, and that is normal. Gemini kept my face closer, ChatGPT got the blazer neater. Keep the one where you still look like yourself.

Tips to get a better result

  • Start with the clearest photo you have. Daylight, face in focus, no harsh shadows across your eyes.
  • Keep it short on the first try. A long prompt muddles a quiet look faster than a loud one.
  • Change one thing at a time. Outfit first, then light, never both together.
  • Use a vertical photo for a profile photo or a print. Most prompts here ask for a 3 to 4 frame for that reason.
  • Check hands, glasses and small details in the result. A second try often fixes them.
  • Save the prompt that worked. The same style will suit another photo later.

Common mistakes to avoid

  • Do not use a dark, blurry or side view photo as your starting photo. The tool will have to guess the face.
  • Do not upload a photo of another person without their permission. Only use photos you have the right to use.
  • Do not copy a prompt and then change every line at once. You will not know what went wrong.
  • Do not give up after one bad run. Try the same prompt once more before you change it.
  • Do not mix two strong styles in one prompt. One clear look per photo works better than a blend of two.

Frequently asked questions

Can I use these prompts for free?

Yes, all 10 prompts on this page are free. Copy any of them, paste it into Gemini or ChatGPT with your own photo, and use the result for your own posts.

Which tool works best with these prompts?

Both work well. Gemini often handles a reference photo nicely, and ChatGPT often follows a detailed prompt nicely. Try the same prompt in both and keep the cleaner result.

Why does the face change in my photo?

The starting photo is usually the cause. A dark, blurry or side view photo makes the tool guess at your face. Use a clear front photo in good light.

Can I change the prompt for my own photo?

Yes. Change the outfit details, the setting or the light to fit your photo, but keep the part about keeping your face the same. Small changes keep the classic feel intact.

Can I use the photo I make for a card or a post?

Yes, for your own personal use. Post it on your own social media or use it as a profile photo. Do not use a photo of another person without their permission.
