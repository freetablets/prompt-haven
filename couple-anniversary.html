Couple Anniversary Prompts: 10 Ideas for 2026

Muhammad AfaqVerified Creator

Published: 2026-11-10 | Couple Prompts | Reading time: about 12 minutes

An anniversary photo should show the years, not just the day. The cake, the old photos on the table, the wedding look worn again, these are the prompts that do that.

You need one clear photo of each of you. The tool places you at home with a small cake, at sunset in matching outfits, or by the sea at dusk, and keeps both faces looking like you.

Some of these look back, like recreating your wedding look or spreading old photos on the table. Some look forward, like toasting with juice glasses on a rooftop at night. Pick the one that fits this year.

They are free. Make one for the card, one for a post, and one to print and keep.

Before you start

An anniversary photo is worth a minute of setup. Five points first.

  • Use one clear photo of each person, both in daylight, with faces in focus and looking at the camera.
  • Pick photos with plain backgrounds, so the tool can place you both in the anniversary scene cleanly.
  • Keep the light in both photos similar, so the two of you blend into one picture.
  • Copy the prompt exactly on the first try, then change one thing at a time.
  • Only use photos of people who are happy for you to use them.

1. At home with a small cake

Create a realistic, natural photo from my uploaded photo. Keep the face exactly the same as in the uploaded photo, with the same eyes, smile, hair and skin tone. Do not change the person into someone else, and do not make the skin look smooth or plastic. Show you as a couple at home with a small anniversary cake, smiling, with soft candle light. Keep the pose relaxed and natural, with a real expression, not a stiff studio pose. Use soft, natural light that suits the scene, with clear detail in the face, hair and clothes. Keep the background simple so the person stays the main focus. Shoot it in a vertical 3 to 4 frame, as if taken on a 35mm lens with a shallow depth of field. The final photo should look like a real photo taken on a good day, with true colours, gentle shadows and a calm, honest feel. It should work well for a profile photo, a print, or a post with family and friends.

How to use these prompts

How to use a prompt in Google Gemini

  1. Open Gemini on your phone or computer.
  2. Upload one clear photo of each person.
  3. Copy one prompt from this page and paste it under the photos.
  4. Send it and wait.
  5. Check both faces first, then the scene. Save the picture you like.

How to use a prompt in ChatGPT

  1. Open ChatGPT on your phone or computer.
  2. Upload both photos in the chat.
  3. Copy one prompt from this page and paste it with the photos.
  4. Send it and wait for the result.
  5. If the setting or the light is not quite right, ask for one small change only.

Anniversary photos mean more when they look like you, so keep the picture that looks most like the two of you, even if another one looks fancier.

Tips to get a better result

  • Use photos taken in similar light, both in daylight if you can. Mixed light makes two faces hard to blend.
  • Pick the scene that fits your year. A quiet year suits the dinner prompt, a big year suits the rooftop.
  • Keep the prompt simple on the first try. The cake and the candles do enough on their own.
  • Check the hands and the glasses in the result. Small objects are where errors show up.
  • Ask for a vertical photo if you want it for a card.
  • Save the prompt that worked. It will give you a matching photo next year.

Common mistakes to avoid

  • Do not use a dark, blurry or side view photo for either person. The tool will guess, and the face will change.
  • Do not use a photo of another person without their permission.
  • Do not change everything in the prompt at once. Change one thing and see what happens.
  • Do not expect the same result every time. Try again if the first picture is not right.
  • Do not add too many details to the scene. A cake, a toast and the two of you are enough.

Frequently asked questions

Can I use these prompts for free?

Yes, all 10 prompts are free. Copy one, paste it into Gemini or ChatGPT with your own photos, and use the picture for a card, a post, or a print.

Which tool works best with these prompts?

Both work well with couple photos. Gemini often handles two reference photos nicely, and ChatGPT often follows a detailed prompt well. Try the same prompt in both and keep the picture you like best.

Why does the face change in my photo?

It usually comes from the starting photos. If one is dark, blurry or taken from the side, the tool guesses that face. Use clear front photos of both people in good light.

Can I change the prompt for my own photo?

Yes. Change the outfits, the setting or the light to suit your photos. Keep the part about keeping both faces the same, and keep the scene simple.

Can I use the photo I make for a card or a post?

Yes, for your own personal use, like an anniversary card, a post on your own social media, or a print. Only use photos of people who are happy for you to use them.
