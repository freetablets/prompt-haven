# Prompt Haven audit

This file records what the original site does, in summary, and what Prompt Haven does differently. No text, prompts, or images were copied from the original site. Only its structure was used as inspiration.

## What the original site does

### Homepage
- A WordPress homepage with a clear title about free AI photo editing prompts, a short welcome paragraph, and a list of recent guides.
- A featured guide at the top, with a short summary and a link to read it.
- A founder and creator strip, with a photo, a short bio, and a job title.
- A FAQ section at the bottom, with short answers about what the site is, how to use a prompt, whether it is free, and which AI tools work best.

### About page
- A long About page that tells a founder story in plain language.
- It explains why the site was started, what problem it solves, and why the prompts are free.
- It introduces the people behind the site, with a short bio for each person, their favourite tools, and their skills.
- It lists a testing promise: prompts are tried many times on real phones before they are published.
- It explains how the site stays free, through simple ads.

### Contact page
- A Contact page with a way to send a message, and notes on what to include in a message.
- It covers custom prompt requests, error reports, and feedback.

### Privacy Policy page
- A long Privacy Policy that covers cookies, ads, analytics, and data use.
- It explains what data is collected, what is not collected, and how a reader can ask for data to be deleted.
- It covers rules for children, and for readers in Europe and California.

### Disclaimer page
- A Disclaimer that covers AI results, no affiliation with AI tools, and safe use of photos.
- It warns that AI results vary, and that a photo of another person should not be used without permission.

### Terms page
- A Terms page that covers free use of prompts, ownership of the site content, and prohibited use.
- It explains what a reader can and cannot do with the prompts and the site text.

### Post structure, in general
- About 96 posts, published from September to November 2026, with 10 to 15 prompts in each post.
- Each post has a title with a year and a number, a short intro, a table of contents, one sample image per prompt, a full copy and paste prompt, a short note on why it works, a Copy button, links to open ChatGPT, Gemini, and Grok, a step by step guide, a FAQ, and related guides.
- 6 pages in total: Home, About, Contact, Privacy, Disclaimer, Terms.
- 4 categories, with clean sitemap and robots files.

## What Prompt Haven does differently

- International audience, not India only. Topics are global, with Christmas and winter added for December.
- Plain static HTML, CSS, and JavaScript, ready for GitHub and Cloudflare Pages. No WordPress, no database, no build step.
- 30 guide posts at launch, not 96. Each post has exactly 10 original prompts.
- One original featured illustration per post, not one sample image per prompt. Each prompt block has a Copy button and links to Gemini, ChatGPT, and Grok.
- All wording, prompts, and images are original, written and made for Prompt Haven.
- Author is Muhammad Afaq, writer and AI prompt creator. No degrees, years of experience, awards, employers, or locations are claimed for him, because none were supplied.
- Contact email is a clear placeholder, `hello@prompt-haven.com`, until the owner gives a real one.
- No AdSense code and no GA4 code on the site yet. Both are listed as still needed, below.
- Dates are spread from 1 October to 20 November 2026 for the 24 non Christmas guides, with 6 Christmas guides dated 21 November to 5 December 2026, ready ahead of December.

## What still needs the site owner

- Real domain name. The site currently uses `prompt-haven.com` as a placeholder in canonical links, sitemap, robots, and llms.txt.
- Real contact email. Replace `hello@prompt-haven.com` on the Contact page and in the footer if needed.
- Real GA4 measurement ID, if analytics are wanted. No GA4 code is on the site yet.
- AdSense code only after approval. Do not add AdSense code before approval, and do not claim approval until it happens.
- More posts to reach 96, like the original site. This launch version has 30 posts. Add new posts in the same format, with the same date and sitemap rules.
- A real GitHub repo name and username, and a Cloudflare Pages project, to deploy the site. Steps are in README.md.
