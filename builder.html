5 tips for a better result

  1. Start with a clear photo taken from the front, in good light. I wasted a week blaming prompts before I realised my starting photos were the problem.
  2. Change one thing at a time. If you change the light, the style and the background all at once, you will never know which one fixed it.
  3. Try the same prompt in both Gemini and ChatGPT. I do this every time, because the same words give me a different photo in each tool, and one of them is usually better.
  4. Ask for soft light when the face matters most. Harsh sun and strong flash are the two things that change a face fastest in my experience.
  5. Save the version you like before you try again. I lost a good one once by pressing send again without saving it, and I still think about that photo.

Builder questions

Is the Prompt Builder free?

Yes, it is free, and there is no sign up. I built it to be free because the whole site is free, and I am not going to charge you for picking five things from a list.

Do I need an account to use it?

No, you do not. You can build and copy as many prompts as you like without an account. You may need an account on Gemini or ChatGPT itself, but that is the tool asking, not me.

Which photo should I start with?

Use a clear photo taken from the front, in good light, where you can see the face properly. I always pick the sharpest one I have, even if it is not my favourite, because a sharp photo keeps the face much closer to the real thing.

Can I use the prompt in any AI tool?

Yes. The prompt it builds works in Gemini, ChatGPT and Grok, and I have tried it in all three. Try Gemini first if you are in a hurry, then ChatGPT with the same photo, and keep the one you like best.
