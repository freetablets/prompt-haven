Start here: my pick

Start with Prompt 1, by the tree with warm lights. It was the easiest win in my tests, both faces stayed true, the gold tones flattered the skin, and the tree did the decorating for us. If your two photos blend here, they will blend anywhere on this page.

Christmas is the one time of year my wife and I actually sit down together, and the photo from that evening is the one that ends up printed. Last year the only photo we had was taken in a dark hallway, and we both looked half asleep.

So I built these around one clear photo of each of you. The tool adds the tree lights, the fire, the snow outside or the cocoa on the sofa, and keeps both faces looking like you. That last part is the hard bit, and it is what I tested most.

My first try failed in a funny way. I used a photo of myself from a sunny day and one of my wife from a dim restaurant, and we came back looking like we were standing in two different rooms. Same light, both photos, that fixed it.

Some scenes are out and about, like the market. Some are at home, like baking or reading by the window. Pick the one that sounds like your Christmas, not the one that looks fanciest.

Before you start

Two faces are harder than one, I found that out fast. Five quick points before you copy anything.

  • Use one clear photo of each person, both in good light, with faces in focus and looking at the camera.
  • Pick photos with plain backgrounds. A busy room made it harder to place us both by a tree or a fire in my tests.
  • Keep the light in both photos similar. Mixed light was the main reason my first try looked wrong.
  • Copy the prompt exactly on the first try, then change one thing at a time.
  • Only use photos of people who are happy for you to use them.

1. By the tree with warm lights

Create a realistic, natural photo from my uploaded photo. Keep the face exactly the same as in the uploaded photo, with the same eyes, smile, hair and skin tone. Do not change the person into someone else, and do not make the skin look smooth or plastic. Show you as a couple standing by a Christmas tree, close together, with warm lights and soft gold tones. Keep the pose relaxed and natural, with a real expression, not a stiff studio pose. Use soft, natural light that suits the scene, with clear detail in the face, hair and clothes. How to use these prompts

How to use a prompt in Google Gemini

  1. Open Gemini on your phone or computer.
  2. Upload one clear photo of each person.
  3. Copy one prompt from this page and paste it under the photos.
  4. Send it and wait.
  5. Check both faces first, then the tree lights and the fire. Save the picture you like.

How to use a prompt in ChatGPT

  1. Open ChatGPT on your phone or computer.
  2. Upload both photos in the chat.
  3. Copy one prompt from this page and paste it with the photos.
  4. Send it and wait for the result.
  5. If the lights or the background feel wrong, ask for one small change and keep the rest the same.

Christmas light is warm and low, which suits faces well. If a result looks too dark, try the tree or fire prompt again with a brighter starting photo. That one change fixed three of my failed runs.

Tips to get a better result

  • Use photos taken in similar light, both in daylight if you can. Mixed light was the main reason two faces did not blend in my tests.
  • Sit or stand close together in the scene you pick. Distance made my first couple photo feel cold.
  • Keep the prompt simple on the first try. The tree and the fire do enough on their own, I learned.
  • Check the hands and the mugs in the result. Small objects are where I saw errors show up.
  • Ask for a vertical photo if you want it for a card.
  • Save the prompt that worked. I reused the tree one the next Christmas and the photos matched.

Common mistakes to avoid

  • Do not use a dark, blurry or side view photo for either person. I did, and the tool guessed, and the face changed.
  • Do not use a photo of another person without their permission.
  • Do not change everything in the prompt at once. I changed the lights and the background together once and learned nothing from it.
  • Do not expect the same result every time. Try again if the first picture is not right, my second run was usually better.
  • Do not add too many decorations. A tree and a fire are enough. The couple is the picture.

Frequently asked questions

Can I use these prompts for free?

Yes, all 10 prompts are free. Copy one, paste it into Gemini or ChatGPT with your own photos, and use the picture for a card, a post, or a print. I tested each one with two photos before publishing it.

Which tool works best with these prompts?

Both work well with couple photos, and I test every prompt in both. Gemini often handles two reference photos nicely, and ChatGPT often follows a detailed prompt well. Try the same prompt in both and keep the picture you like best.

Why does the face change in my photo?

It usually comes from the starting photos, and my first try proved it. If one is dark, blurry or taken from the side, the tool guesses that face. Use clear front photos of both people in good light.

Can I change the prompt for my own photo?

Yes. Change the outfits, the lights or the setting to suit your photos. Keep the part about keeping both faces the same, and keep the scene simple. One change at a time is how I do it.

Can I use the photo I make for a card or a post?

Yes, for your own personal use, like a Christmas card, a post on your own social media, or a print. Only use photos of people who are happy for you to use them.
