Start here: my pick

Try Prompt 2 first, laughing over coffee. My small table try looked nice but a bit stiff, like we were posing for a menu. The laughing one relaxed both our faces at once, and the cafe just faded into the background where it belongs.

Every couple has a cafe. The one with the good coffee, the corner table, and the waiter who knows your order. These prompts are for that place.

You need one clear photo of each of you. The tool puts you at a small table with coffee, by the window, or in a cosy corner, and keeps both faces looking like you.

My first try failed because our photos were taken years apart, one in summer sun, one in a dark room. The tool could not blend them. Two daylight photos fixed it.

Some scenes are quiet, like reading together. Some are loud, like laughing over coffee. Pick the one that sounds like your usual date, not the one that sounds impressive.

Before you start

Two faces are harder than one, so the setup matters. Five quick points first, then you can copy a prompt.

  • Use one clear photo of each person, both in daylight, with faces in focus and looking at the camera.
  • Pick photos with plain backgrounds if you can. A busy background makes it harder to place you both in a cafe.
  • Keep the light in both photos similar, so the two of you blend into one scene.
  • Copy the prompt exactly on the first try, then change one thing at a time.
  • Only use photos of people who are happy for you to use them.

1. At a small table with coffee

Create a realistic, natural photo from my uploaded photo. Keep the face exactly the same as in the uploaded photo, with the same eyes, smile, hair and skin tone. Do not change the person into someone else, and do not make the skin look smooth or plastic. Show you as a couple at a small cafe table with coffee cups, smiling, with warm indoor light. Keep the pose relaxed and natural, with a real expression, not a stiff studio pose. Use soft, natural light that suits the scene, with clear detail in the face, hair and clothes. Keep the background simple so the person stays the main focus. Shoot it in a vertical 3 to 4 frame, as if taken on a 35mm lens with a shallow depth of field. The final photo should look like a real photo taken on a good day, with true colours, gentle shadows and a calm, honest feel. It should work well for a profile photo, a print, or a post with family and friends.

How to use these prompts

How to use a prompt in Google Gemini

  1. Open Gemini on your phone or computer.
  2. Upload one clear photo of each person.
  3. Copy one prompt from this page and paste it under the photos.
  4. Send it and wait.
  5. Check both faces first, then the cups and the table. Save the picture you like.

How to use a prompt in ChatGPT

  1. Open ChatGPT on your phone or computer.
  2. Upload both photos in the chat.
  3. Copy one prompt from this page and paste it with the photos.
  4. Send it and wait for the result.
  5. If the cafe or the light looks wrong, ask for one small change and keep the rest the same.

Cafe light is warm and low, which suits faces. If a result looks too dark, try the window prompt with a brighter starting photo. That one saved my own set.

Tips to get a better result

  • Use photos taken in similar light, both in daylight if you can. Mixed light makes two faces hard to blend.
  • Sit close together in the scene you pick. A couple photo needs closeness.
  • Keep the prompt simple on the first try. The cups and the table do enough on their own.
  • Check the hands and the cups in the result. Small objects are where errors show up.
  • Ask for a vertical photo if you want it for a post or a phone screen.
  • Save the prompt that worked. It will give you a matching photo next time.

Common mistakes to avoid

  • Do not use a dark, blurry or side view photo for either person. The tool will guess, and the face will change.
  • Do not use a photo of another person without their permission.
  • Do not change everything in the prompt at once. Change the table or the light, not both.
  • Do not rewrite the prompt after one try. Run it again first.
  • Do not add too many cafe details. Two cups, a table and the two of you are enough.

Frequently asked questions

Can I use these prompts for free?

Yes, all 10 prompts are free. Copy one, paste it into Gemini or ChatGPT with your own photos, and use the picture for a post, a print, or just for yourselves.

Which tool works best with these prompts?

Both work well with couple photos. Gemini often handles two reference photos nicely, and ChatGPT often follows a detailed prompt well. Try the same prompt in both and keep the picture you like best.

Why does the face change in my photo?

It usually comes from the starting photos. If one is dark, blurry or taken from the side, the tool guesses that face. Use clear front photos of both people in good light.

Can I change the prompt for my own photo?

Yes. Change the table, the drinks or the setting to suit your photos. Keep the part about keeping both faces the same, and keep the scene simple.

Can I use the photo I make for a card or a post?

Yes, for your own personal use, like a post on your own social media or a print. Only use photos of people who are happy for you to use them.
