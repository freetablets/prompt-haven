Mirror Selfie Prompts: 10 Ideas for Gemini and ChatGPT 2026

Muhammad Afaq Verified Creator
Published: 2026-10-17 | Trending Prompts | Reading time: about 12 minutes
Start here: my pick

Start with Prompt 1, the bright bedroom. I tried it with a mirror photo I had almost deleted, a plain white tee and messy hair, and it came back clean. The mirror was clear, the light was soft, and for once my phone was not covering half my face.

I have taken more mirror selfies than I can count, and most of them have the same three problems. A shadow across my face, a messy room behind me, or my phone eating half the frame. I wrote these prompts to fix exactly those three things.

The first one I tested was a lift mirror photo from a shopping trip. Bad idea. The lift light was yellow, the background was a metal door, and the result looked like a stranger in a lift. I switched to a bedroom photo with daylight from the window and it was a different story.

What surprised me was how much the starting photo matters here. More than any other guide on this site, I think. If your face is half hidden by the phone, no prompt will put it back. Pick the photo where you can actually see yourself.

Try one scene first. A bright bedroom or a hallway mirror is the easiest place to start, and the result will tell you in seconds whether your photo is good enough to work with.

Before you start

Mirror photos are picky, so a quick check before you start saves a lot of retries.

  • Use a mirror photo where your face is visible and the light is decent. If the phone covers your face, pick a different photo.
  • Wipe the mirror in the starting photo if you can. Smudges turn into strange marks in the result, I found that out the hard way.
  • Keep the background tidy in your starting photo. A messy room behind you stays messy in the result.
  • Copy the prompt exactly on the first try, then change one thing at a time.
  • Only use a photo of yourself, or of someone who is happy for you to use it.

10 copy and paste prompts

Find the mirror scene that matches your photo, copy the prompt, and paste it with the photo. If the mirror in your photo is small, pick a scene with a big mirror.

1. In a bright bedroom

Create a realistic, natural photo from my uploaded photo. Keep the face exactly the same as in the uploaded photo, with the same eyes, smile, hair and skin tone. Do not change the person into someone else, and do not make the skin look smooth or plastic. Show you taking a mirror selfie in a bright bedroom, in a simple outfit, with soft daylight. Keep the pose relaxed and natural, with a real expression, not a stiff studio pose. Use soft, natural light that suits the scene, with clear detail in the face, hair and clothes. Keep the background simple so the person stays the main focus. Shoot it in a vertical 3 to 4 frame, as if taken on a 35mm lens with a shallow depth of field. The final photo should look like a real photo taken on a good day, with true colours, gentle shadows and a calm, honest feel. It should work well for a profile photo, a print, or a post with family and friends.

ChatGPTGeminiGrok
Why this works

A bright bedroom is the easiest mirror scene there is. Daylight from the window softens your face, and a tidy room keeps the focus on you, not on the clutter.

How to use these prompts

Style photos are about a look, so keep the starting photo simple and let the prompt add the clothes, the setting and the mood.

How to use a prompt in Google Gemini

  1. Open Gemini on your phone or computer.
  2. Upload a clear photo. Your face should be in focus and facing the camera.
  3. Copy one prompt from this page and paste it under your photo.
  4. Send it, and wait for the result. Do not send the same prompt again while it is still working.
  5. Look at the face first, then the outfit and the background. If the face looks right but the style feels too strong, try the same prompt once more.

How to use a prompt in ChatGPT

  1. Open ChatGPT on your phone or computer.
  2. Upload your photo in the chat.
  3. Copy one prompt from this page and paste it with your photo.
  4. Send it, and wait for the result.
  5. If the result is close but not quite right, ask for one small change, such as softer light or a plainer background, and keep the rest of the prompt the same.

If the mirror in your result looks warped or the phone looks wrong, do not keep tweaking the prompt. Swap in a clearer starting photo first. Nine times out of ten, that was my problem, not the words.

Tips to get a better result

  • Use the clearest mirror photo you have. If your face is half covered by the phone, the tool will invent the other half.
  • Stand in daylight if you can. Window light fixed more of my mirror photos than any prompt change did.
  • Start with the bedroom or hallway prompts. They are the most forgiving, because the light is simple and the background is plain.
  • Check the mirror edges and your hands in the result. That is where the weird bits show up first.
  • Keep your outfit simple in the starting photo. A busy pattern can come back changed, and then the whole photo feels off.
  • Save the prompt that worked. The same mirror scene will suit a different outfit next time, and you will not have to start from scratch.

Common mistakes to avoid

  • Do not use a photo where the phone covers most of your face. The tool will guess, and the guess will look like someone else.
  • Do not use a dark lift or a yellow-lit room as your starting photo. The colour follows you into the result.
  • Do not change everything in the prompt at once. Change one thing, the room or the light, and see what happens.
  • Do not expect the mirror to stay perfect. I had one result where the mirror frame bent like rubber. Try again with a plainer scene.
  • Do not add a busy background to a mirror selfie. The mirror already has a lot going on. Keep the room simple.

Frequently asked questions

Can I use these prompts for free?

Yes, all 10 prompts are free, plus the 3 bonus ones further down the page. Copy one, paste it into Gemini or ChatGPT with your mirror photo, and use the picture for a post or a print.

Which tool works best with these prompts?

Both work well, and I use both. Gemini kept my face closer on the bedroom prompt, while ChatGPT handled the cafe scene better. Run the same prompt in both and keep the picture you like best.

Why does the face change in my photo?

It is almost always the starting photo. If it is dark, blurry, or taken from the side, or if your phone covers your face, the tool has to guess. Use a clear front photo in good light and leave the prompt alone on the first try.

Can I change the prompt for my own photo?

Yes. Change the room, the outfit, or the light to fit your photo. Keep the line about keeping the face the same, and keep the scene simple so the tool has less to get wrong.

Can I use the photo I make for a card or a post?

Yes, for your own personal use, like a post on your own social media or a print. Only use a photo of someone else if they are happy for you to use it.
