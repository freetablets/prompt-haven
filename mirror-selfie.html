Mirror Selfie Prompts: 10 Ideas for Gemini and ChatGPT 2026

Muhammad Afaq Verified Creator
Published: 2026-10-17 | Trending Prompts | Reading time: about 12 minutes

Almost everyone has taken a mirror selfie. In a bedroom, in a lift, in a shop changing room. Some of them turn out great. Most of them have a weird shadow, a messy background, or a phone covering half your face.

These prompts clean that up. They keep your face and your outfit the same, and place you in front of a clear mirror with good light and a tidy background.

Start with a mirror photo where your face is visible and the light is decent. The prompt cannot rescue a photo that is mostly phone and shadow, so pick your clearest one.

Try one scene first. A bright bedroom or a hallway mirror is the easiest place to start, and the result will tell you quickly if your photo is good enough.

Before you start

Mirror photos have their own quirks, so run through these five points before you copy a prompt.

  • Pick a photo where your face is clear, not hidden behind your phone. Hold the phone low or to one side if you take a fresh one.
  • Use a photo with decent light on your face. A mirror in a dark room will give you a dark result, however good the prompt is.
  • Wipe the mirror and keep the background tidy in your starting photo. The tool copies what it sees, clutter included.
  • Copy the prompt exactly as it is the first time. Change one thing later if you need to, not five at once.
  • Save the best result, then try once more. Mirror light shifts a little each run, and the second version is often the cleaner one.

10 copy and paste prompts

Pick the mirror scene that matches your photo best, copy the prompt, and paste it in.

1. In a bright bedroom

Create a realistic, natural photo from my uploaded photo. Keep the face exactly the same as in the uploaded photo, with the same eyes, smile, hair and skin tone. Do not change the person into someone else, and do not make the skin look smooth or plastic. Show you taking a mirror selfie in a bright bedroom, in a simple outfit, with soft daylight. Keep the pose relaxed and natural, with a real expression, not a stiff studio pose. Use soft, natural light that suits the scene, with clear detail in the face, hair and clothes. Keep the background simple so the person stays the main focus. Shoot it in a vertical 3 to 4 frame, as if taken on a 35mm lens with a shallow depth of field. The final photo should look like a real photo taken on a good day, with true colours, gentle shadows and a calm, honest feel. It should work well for a profile photo, a print, or a post with family and friends.

ChatGPTGeminiGrok
Why this works

A bright bedroom is the easiest mirror scene to believe. Daylight, a clean wall, your real outfit. Nothing in the frame feels staged.

How to use these prompts

Style photos are about a look, so keep the starting photo simple and let the prompt add the clothes, the setting and the mood.

How to use a prompt in Google Gemini

  1. Open Gemini on your phone or computer.
  2. Upload a clear photo. Your face should be in focus and facing the camera.
  3. Copy one prompt from this page and paste it under your photo.
  4. Send it, and wait for the result. Do not send the same prompt again while it is still working.
  5. Look at the face first, then the outfit and the background. If the face looks right but the style feels too strong, try the same prompt once more.

How to use a prompt in ChatGPT

  1. Open ChatGPT on your phone or computer.
  2. Upload your photo in the chat.
  3. Copy one prompt from this page and paste it with your photo.
  4. Send it, and wait for the result.
  5. If the result is close but not quite right, ask for one small change, such as softer light or a plainer background, and keep the rest of the prompt the same.

Both tools can give a different result from the same prompt. That is normal. Try the same prompt in both tools if you have time, and keep the photo you like best.

Tips to get a better result

  • Start with the clearest photo you have. A photo taken in daylight, with the face in focus, gives the best result.
  • Keep the prompt simple on the first try. Long, complex prompts can confuse the tool and change the face.
  • Change one thing at a time. If you want a different outfit, change only the outfit. If you want different light, change only the light.
  • Use a vertical photo for a profile photo or a print. Most of the prompts on this page ask for a vertical 3 to 4 frame for that reason.
  • Check hands, glasses, hats and small details in the result. AI tools still make small errors there, and a second try often fixes them.
  • Save the prompt that worked for you. If you use the same prompt again with a new photo, the style will stay similar.

Common mistakes to avoid

  • Do not use a dark, blurry or side view photo as your starting photo. The tool will have to guess the face, and the result can look like a different person.
  • Do not upload a photo of another person without their permission. Only use photos you have the right to use.
  • Do not copy a prompt and then change every line at once. You will not know what went wrong if the result is poor.
  • Do not expect the same result every time. AI tools vary, even with the same prompt and the same photo.
  • Do not mix two strong styles in one prompt. One clear look per photo works better than a blend of two.

Frequently asked questions

Can I use these prompts for free?

Yes, all 10 prompts on this page are free. Copy any of them, paste it into Gemini or ChatGPT with your own photo, and use the result for your own posts.

Which tool works best with these prompts?

Both work well. Gemini often handles a reference photo nicely, and ChatGPT often follows a detailed prompt nicely. Try the same prompt in both and keep the cleaner result.

Why does the face change in my photo?

The starting photo is usually the cause. A dark, blurry or side view photo makes the tool guess at your face, and so does a phone covering most of it. Use a clear photo with your face in view.

Can I change the prompt for my own photo?

Yes. Change the outfit, the mirror setting or the light to fit your photo, but keep the part about keeping your face the same. One change at a time is the safe way.

Can I use the photo I make for a card or a post?

Yes, for your own personal use. Post it on your own social media or use it as a profile photo. Do not use a photo of another person without their permission.
