Before you start

Two faces are harder than one, so the setup matters. Five quick points first, then the fun starts.

  • Use one clear photo of each person, both taken in daylight, with faces in focus and looking at the camera.
  • Pick photos with plain backgrounds if you can. A busy background makes it harder to place you both on a beach.
  • Keep the light in both photos similar. One sunny photo and one dark photo will not blend well.
  • Copy the prompt exactly on the first try. Change one thing at a time after that.
  • Only use photos of people who are happy for you to use them.

1. Walking on the sand at sunset

Create a realistic, natural photo from my uploaded photo. Keep the face exactly the same as in the uploaded photo, with the same eyes, smile, hair and skin tone. Do not change the person into someone else, and do not make the skin look smooth or plastic. Show you as a couple walking on sand at sunset, holding hands, with warm light and soft waves. Keep the pose relaxed and natural, with a real expression, not a stiff studio pose. Use soft, natural light that suits the scene, with clear detail in the face, hair and clothes. The final photo should look like a real photo taken on a good day, with true colours, gentle shadows and a calm, honest feel. It should work well for a profile photo, a print, or a post with family and friends.

How to use these prompts

How to use a prompt in Google Gemini

  1. Open Gemini on your phone or computer.
  2. Upload one clear photo of each person.
  3. Copy one prompt from this page and paste it under the photos.
  4. Send it and wait.
  5. Check both faces first. If one face looks wrong, try again with a clearer photo of that person.

How to use a prompt in ChatGPT

  1. Open ChatGPT on your phone or computer.
  2. Upload both photos in the chat.
  3. Copy one prompt from this page and paste it with the photos.
  4. Send it and wait for the result.
  5. If the light or the sea looks off, ask for one small change and keep the rest the same.

Two faces are harder than one, so expect a retry or two. The second try is often the one that looks like both of you. Mine was, and it is the one we printed.

Tips to get a better result

  • Use photos taken on the same kind of day, both in daylight. Mixed light makes blending harder.
  • Smile the way you normally do together. A stiff pose is easy to spot.
  • Keep the prompt simple on the first try. Long prompts can change a face.
  • Ask for a vertical photo if you want it as a phone wallpaper or a print.
  • Check the hands and the feet in the result. Those are the spots where errors creep in.
  • Save the prompt that worked. It will keep the same feel if you use it again.

Common mistakes to avoid

  • Do not use a dark, blurry or side view photo for either person. The tool will guess, and the face will change.
  • Do not use a photo of another person without their permission.
  • Do not change everything in the prompt at once. You will not know what went wrong.
  • Do not give up after one odd result. The same prompt can come out differently on the next run.
  • Do not add too many details to the scene. A crowded beach hides the couple.

Frequently asked questions

Can I use these prompts for free?

Yes, all 10 prompts are free. Copy one, paste it into Gemini or ChatGPT with your own photos, and use the picture for yourselves, for a post, or for a print.

Which tool works best with these prompts?

Both work well with couple photos. Gemini often handles two reference photos nicely, and ChatGPT often follows a detailed prompt well. Try the same prompt in both and keep the picture you like best.

Why does the face change in my photo?

It usually comes from the starting photos. If one is dark, blurry or taken from the side, the tool guesses that face. Use clear front photos of both people in good light.

Can I change the prompt for my own photo?

Yes. Change the outfits, the time of day or the background to suit your photos. Keep the part about keeping both faces the same, and keep the scene simple.

Can I use the photo I make for a card or a post?

Yes, for your own personal use, like a post on your own social media or a print at home. Only use photos of people who are happy for you to use them.
