How it works

1

Pick a guide

I usually start with the photo, not the guide. Find the one that matches what you have, a Christmas family shot or a wedding photo, and look through its 10 prompts until one sounds right.

2

Copy a prompt

Tap the Copy prompt button under the one you like. I still double check it landed on my clipboard, old habit, then I paste it straight into the AI tool.

3

Paste it with your photo

Open Gemini or ChatGPT, upload a clear photo, paste the prompt, and send it. My first try is rarely perfect, so I save the one I like best and try again with a small tweak.

Frequently asked questions

Are the prompts really free?

Yes, they are. Every prompt on this site is free to copy and use with your own photos, as many times as you like. I have copied the same one three times in one evening while testing, and nothing asked me to pay.

Do I need to sign up?

No, you do not. You can read and copy prompts here without an account. You may need an account on Gemini or ChatGPT itself, but that is the tool asking, not me.

Which tool should I use?

I use both, because the same prompt can give you a different photo in each one. Try Gemini first if you are in a hurry, then try ChatGPT with the same photo, and keep the one you like best.

Why does the face change sometimes?

It usually comes down to the photo you start with, I learned this after blaming the prompt for a week. A clear photo taken from the front in good light keeps the face much closer to the real thing, so try a clearer photo before you change the prompt.
