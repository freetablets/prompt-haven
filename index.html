Frequently asked questions

Are the prompts really free?

Yes, they are. Every prompt on this site is free to copy and use with your own photos, as many times as you like. There is no paywall hiding behind the first few prompts.

Do I need to sign up?

No, you do not. You can read and copy prompts without an account here. You may need an account on Gemini or ChatGPT itself, but that is the tool's rule, not ours.

Which tool should I use?

Both Gemini and ChatGPT work well, and they often give different results from the same prompt. Try your prompt in both and keep the photo you like best.

Why does the face change sometimes?

It usually comes down to the photo you start with. A clear photo taken from the front in good light keeps the face much closer to the real thing. If the face looks off, try a clearer photo before you change the prompt.
