How it works

  1. Pick a guide. Choose a guide that fits your photo, such as a Christmas family photo, a wedding photo, or a baby photo.
  2. Copy a prompt. Click Copy prompt under the prompt you like. The full prompt is copied, ready to paste.
  3. Paste it with your photo. Open Gemini or ChatGPT, upload a clear photo, paste the prompt, and send it. Save the result you like best.

Why it is free

Prompt Haven is free to read because we want good prompts to be open to everyone, not hidden behind a paywall. There is no sign up and no subscription. In the future, the site may show simple ads through Google AdSense, once it is approved. Ads will not stop you from copying a prompt, and we will never sell your personal data.

Frequently asked questions

Are the prompts really free?

Yes. Every prompt on this site is free. You can copy and use as many as you like for your own photos.

Do I need to sign up?

No. You do not need an account on Prompt Haven. You only need an account on the AI tool you use, such as Gemini or ChatGPT, if that tool asks for one.

Which tool should I use?

Google Gemini and ChatGPT both work well. Try the same prompt in both tools and keep the result you like best. Results vary by tool and by photo.

Why does the face change sometimes?

Use a clear front photo in good light. A dark, blurry, or side view photo makes the face harder to keep the same. Try again with a clearer photo.
