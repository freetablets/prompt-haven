Contact Prompt Haven

Last updated: 20 November 2026

I would love to hear from you. If a guide helped you, if a prompt did not work, or if you have an idea for a new guide, send me a message at the email below. Tell me what happened in your own words and I will take a look.

Email us at hello@prompt-haven.com

You can also write to the site writer, Muhammad Afaq, at afaq@prompt-haven.com. Both addresses reach us, and we read every message.

What to write in your message

To help us reply faster, please include these details in your message.

  • The name of the guide you were reading, for example Christmas Family Photo Prompts.
  • The number of the prompt, if your message is about one prompt.
  • Which tool you used, for example Google Gemini or ChatGPT.
  • What went wrong, in your own words. For example, the face changed, the hands looked wrong, or the light was too dark.
  • What you would like us to do. For example, fix a prompt, add a new guide, or correct a link.

Request a custom prompt

You can ask us for a prompt for a photo idea that is not on the site yet. Tell us who is in the photo, where it was taken, and what kind of photo you want. For example, a family photo in a garden, a couple photo at the beach, or a birthday photo for a child. We will read your request and, if it fits the site, we may turn it into a full guide with 10 prompts. We cannot promise to make every requested guide, and we cannot promise a date, but we do read every request.

Report an error

If you find a broken link, a spelling error, a prompt that does not make sense, or a photo that does not load, please tell us. Include the page address and a short note about what you saw. We will check it and fix it.

Report a problem with a photo or a prompt

If you think a prompt or an image on this site is wrong, unsafe, or copied from another site, please tell us at once. Include the page address and a short note. We will look at it and remove or fix it if needed.

What we cannot do by email

We cannot edit your photo for you, and we cannot make a photo for you by email. The prompts on this site are for you to use yourself, in Gemini or ChatGPT, with your own photo. We also cannot give advice about the AI tools themselves, such as account problems or billing. For those problems, please contact the tool you use.

How long does a reply take?

We aim to reply within a few working days. Please do not send the same message many times. One clear message is enough.

Thank you for taking the time to write to Prompt Haven.
