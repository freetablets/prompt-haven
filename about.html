About Prompt Haven

Last updated: 20 November 2026

Prompt Haven is a free website for people who want better photos from AI tools. You will find copy and paste prompts here for Google Gemini, ChatGPT, and other AI photo tools. Copy any prompt, paste it with your own photo, and make a photo for a card, a print, a profile photo, or a post with family and friends.

I started Prompt Haven with a simple idea. Many people have a good photo on their phone, but they do not know what to write to turn it into a photo they love. Some prompt sites hide their best prompts behind a paywall, and others share short, vague prompts that do not work well. I wanted a clean, free place where the prompts are written in full, tested with real photos, and easy to use, even if you have never used an AI photo tool before.

What we do here

Each guide on Prompt Haven has 10 prompts. Each prompt is written in full, with a clear scene, simple light, and a note about keeping the face the same as in your photo. After each prompt, there is a short note that explains why it works, in plain words. Each guide also has a step by step guide, tips, and answers to common questions. We do this because a prompt on its own is not enough. You also need to know how to use it, what photo to start with, and what to do if the first result is not right.

We cover photos for many parts of life. There are prompts for Christmas and winter, for couples and weddings, for maternity and babies, for birthdays, for autumn and spring, for travel, for graduation, and for everyday portraits. We add new guides for the season that is coming, so the prompts are ready when you need them. In December, for example, Christmas prompts are the most useful, so we publish them in November, before the month starts.

All the prompts on this site are original. We write them ourselves, in our own words. We do not copy prompts, text, or images from other sites. The sample images on this site are original illustrations made for Prompt Haven. They are there to show the mood of a guide, not to claim that a photo is a real photo of a real person.

Meet the writer

Muhammad Afaq

Muhammad Afaq is the writer and AI prompt creator at Prompt Haven. He writes the prompts on this site and tests them using Google Gemini and ChatGPT, with real photos, to check that each prompt is clear, simple, and easy to copy and paste.

When he tests a prompt, he starts with a clear photo, copies the prompt as it is written on the page, and looks at the result with care. He checks the face first, then the hands, the background, and the light. If a prompt changes the face, or if it makes the skin look smooth and unreal, he rewrites it until it is clearer. Only then does it go on the site.

Muhammad Afaq writes for people who are not experts in AI. He keeps the language simple, the sentences short, and the steps clear. He does not use long, complex words when a plain word will do. His aim is simple: a reader should be able to open a guide, copy a prompt, and make a photo in a few minutes, without needing to learn how AI tools work in detail.

How we test a prompt

We do not publish a prompt just because it sounds good. Before a guide goes live, each prompt is checked in a simple, honest way. First, we read the prompt out loud and ask if a new reader would understand it. If a line is unclear, we rewrite it. Second, we try the prompt with a real photo in Gemini and ChatGPT. We look at the face, the expression, the hands, and the background. Third, we try it a second time, because AI tools can give a different result from the same prompt. If a prompt works well on the first try but fails on the second, we make it clearer and try again.

We also check that a prompt does not ask for anything that would harm a person or break the rules of the AI tool. We do not write prompts that change a person into someone else, that make a fake photo of a real person without permission, or that use a photo of a child in a way that is not safe. You can read more about this in our Disclaimer and Terms pages.

Our promise to readers

  • All prompts on Prompt Haven are free, now and in the future. There is no paywall, no subscription, and no need to sign up to read a prompt.
  • We write in plain, human language. We do not fill a page with long words or filler just to make it look longer.
  • We say clearly when results vary. AI photo tools do not give the same result every time, and we will not promise a perfect photo.
  • We respect your photos. We do not ask you to upload a photo to this site. You upload your photo only to the AI tool you choose, and that tool has its own privacy rules.
  • We fix errors when we find them. If a prompt is unclear, or if a link is broken, we will correct it.

How Prompt Haven stays free

Prompt Haven is free to read. In the future, we may show simple ads through Google AdSense, once the site is approved. We will not hide prompts behind ads, and we will not ask you to pay for a prompt. If ads appear on the site one day, they will be clearly marked as ads, and they will not stop you from copying a prompt. We will never sell your personal data. You can read more about ads, cookies, and data in our Privacy Policy.

Contact us

If you have an idea for a guide, if a prompt does not work for you, or if you spot an error, please tell us. Use the Contact page to send a message. Tell us which guide you were reading, what photo you used, and what went wrong, and we will look at it. We read every message, and we use your feedback to make the guides better.

Thank you for reading Prompt Haven. We hope the prompts help you make a photo you are happy to share.
