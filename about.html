About Prompt Haven

Last updated: 20 November 2026

I made Prompt Haven as a free website for people who want better photos from AI tools. You will find copy and paste prompts here for Google Gemini, ChatGPT, and other AI photo tools. Copy any prompt, paste it with your own photo, and make a photo for a card, a print, a profile photo, or a post with family and friends.

I started Prompt Haven with a simple idea, and it came from my own phone. I had a good photo, but I did not know what to write to turn it into a photo I loved. Some prompt sites hid their best prompts behind a paywall, and others shared short, vague prompts that did not work well. I wanted a clean, free place where the prompts are written in full, tested with real photos, and easy to use, even if you have never used an AI photo tool before.

Why I made this site

I made this site because of one photo. It was my mum, laughing in the garden on a summer evening, and the light was perfect, but the photo itself was a mess, tilted, a bit dark, half of her face in shadow. I wanted to fix it without learning photo editing, and I typed the first thing that came into my head into an AI tool. The result looked nothing like her. I tried again the next night, and the night after that, and slowly I learned what to write and what to leave out. That is the whole reason this site exists. I keep a note of every prompt that works, I test it with real photos of my own family, and I put it here so you do not have to spend three evenings the way I did. I am not a photographer and I do not pretend to be one. I am just a person who likes his photos and wants yours to turn out well too.

What we do here

Each guide on Prompt Haven has 10 prompts. I write each prompt in full, with a clear scene, simple light, and a note about keeping the face the same as in your photo. After each prompt, there is a short note that explains why it works, in plain words. Each guide also has a step by step guide, tips, and answers to common questions. I do this because a prompt on its own is not enough. You also need to know how to use it, what photo to start with, and what to do if the first result is not right.

I cover photos for many parts of life. There are prompts for Christmas and winter, for couples and weddings, for maternity and babies, for birthdays, for autumn and spring, for travel, for graduation, and for everyday portraits. I add new guides for the season that is coming, so the prompts are ready when you need them. In December, for example, Christmas prompts are the most useful, so I publish them in the autumn, well before December starts.

All the prompts on this site are original. I write them myself, in my own words. I do not copy prompts, text, or images from other sites. The sample images on this site are original illustrations made for Prompt Haven. They are there to show the mood of a guide, not to claim that a photo is a real photo of a real person.

How we test a prompt

I do not publish a prompt just because it sounds good. Before a guide goes live, I check each prompt in a simple, honest way. First, I read the prompt out loud and ask if a new reader would understand it. If a line is unclear, I rewrite it. Second, I try the prompt with a real photo in Gemini and ChatGPT. I look at the face, the expression, the hands, and the background. Third, I try it a second time, because AI tools can give a different result from the same prompt. If a prompt works well on the first try but fails on the second, I make it clearer and try again.

I also check that a prompt does not ask for anything that would harm a person or break the rules of the AI tool. I do not write prompts that change a person into someone else, that make a fake photo of a real person without permission, or that use a photo of a child in a way that is not safe. You can read more about this in our Disclaimer and Terms pages.

Our promise to readers

  • I keep all prompts on Prompt Haven free, now and in the future. There is no paywall, no subscription, and no need to sign up to read a prompt.
  • I write in plain, human language. I do not fill a page with long words or filler just to make it look longer.
  • I say clearly when results vary. AI photo tools do not give the same result every time, and I will not promise a perfect photo.
  • I respect your photos. I do not ask you to upload a photo to this site. You upload your photo only to the AI tool you choose, and that tool has its own privacy rules.
  • I fix errors when I find them. If a prompt is unclear, or if a link is broken, I will correct it.

How Prompt Haven stays free

Prompt Haven is free to read. In the future, I may show simple ads through Google AdSense, once the site is approved. I will not hide prompts behind ads, and I will not ask you to pay for a prompt. If ads appear on the site one day, they will be clearly marked as ads, and they will not stop you from copying a prompt. I will never sell your personal data. You can read more about ads, cookies, and data in our Privacy Policy.
